# Crimson Vision Studio

Build a premium, high-converting dark-mode designer portfolio website inspired by modern minimalist agency designs (React, Tailwind CSS, Lucide icons).

1. VISUAL DESIGN & THEME:

- Aesthetic: Modern dark mode with rich red accent accents, subtle card elevation, clean grid borders, and smooth glowing hover states.

- Color Palette: 

  * Background: Pure Pitch Dark / Charcoal Black (#0A0A0A)

  * Primary Accent / Highlights: Bright Crimson Red (#FF2A3C) for CTA buttons, pill badges, and key highlight text.

  * Card Surfaces: Deep Charcoal (#141414) with subtle muted gray borders.

  * Typography: Bold sans-serif typography with strong contrast (Pure White headers, Muted Gray body text).

2. KEY SECTIONS & CONTENT LAYOUT:

- Sticky Floating Header: Compact dark pill navbar floating at the top with a pill badge logo, navigation links ("Work", "Services", "Contact"), and a prominent red CTA button "Book a call with me".

- Hero Section: 

  * Massive typography layout: "THINK CREATIVELY" with "CREATIVELY" highlighted in vivid red.

  * Left subtext: "I help brands turn ideas into structured, meaningful experiences".

  * Right column visual: High-quality 3D avatar head illustration cutout overlapping the header area.

  * Stat Bar / Social Proof: Below hero, display "5 star rating - 35+ Happy Clients" alongside logo wall placeholders (e.g., Hoodverse, Tea Sense, Fruit Blends).

- Latest Projects Showcase:

  * Section title "Latest Projects".

  * Grid of modern project cards featuring visual previews (e.g., "HOODVERSE - Branding & Visual Identity", "Tea Sense - A Premium Tea", "Fruit Blends").

  * Hover effects: Cards smoothly lift and reveal tags like "View Project" and category badges ("Packaging Design", "Visual Identity").

  * Floating persistent widget at bottom right: "Speak to me - Email or book a call" floating contact pill.

- Testimonial Highlight: Dark card displaying a quote with red quote marks: '"Ruchit's approach is structured. He focuses on clarity before designing..."' with client avatar and title.

- Interactive Capability / Services Section ("What I help you to Shape..."):

  * Left sidebar: "Tools that I use" displaying software icons (Ps, Ai, Ae, Figma, Blender, Spline, Id).

  * Right list: Interactive expandable accordions/cards for services:

    1. Design Strategy

    2. Brand Identity (with tags: Logo Design, Visual Identity Systems, Typography, Brand Guidelines)

    3. UI/UX Design (with tags: Wireframing, Prototyping, Usability Testing, Interaction Design)

    4. Packaging Design & Digital Design.

- About & Experience Section ("Designing experiences that make sense"):

  * Professional portrait photo placeholder alongside an engaging narrative bio.

  * "My work history" card detailing roles, companies, and timelines.

- Client Testimonial Carousel: Grid/carousel of high-rating client feedback cards highlighting results and brand impact.

- FAQ & Discovery Call Banner:

  * Left: Accordion list of numbered FAQs (01. How do we get started?, 02. Will I be involved in the design process?, 03. How many changes can I request?, etc.).

  * Right: Highlighted crimson red CTA card: "Still not sure? Book a free discovery call." with dual action buttons ("Schedule Now" and "Convert").

- Footer: Big bold text "Lets build incredible work together.", displaying direct contact email, booking link, social links, and large background watermark typography.

3. INTERACTIVITY & UX POLISH:

- Fully mobile & tablet responsive layout.

- Micro-interactions: Smooth accordion expansion for FAQs and Services, hover zoom on project thumbnails, smooth scrolling on anchor links.

- Interactive floating call widget at the bottom corner that stays fixed as the user scrolls.
